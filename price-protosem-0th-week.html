PRICE Protosem — Our 0th Week

17–21 August 2026 • 5 Days. 5 Experiences. 1 New Perspective.

“We didn’t just learn entrepreneurship. We experienced the mindset.”

5-Day Mindset Progression
Connect Discover Collaborate Own Create

PRICE Protosem’s first batch began its journey with five immersive days of learning, reflection, games, teamwork, ownership, and entrepreneurial inspiration. Each day brought a new experience—and together they shaped one new perspective.

Day 1 17 August 2026

Connect

Break the Ice. Build the Connection.

Our first day was about introductions, first hellos, and building comfort with one another. We got to know PRICE Protosem, the programme, and the people beginning this journey alongside us.

A simple Rock–Paper–Scissors tournament got us talking, laughing, competing, and connecting across diverse backgrounds.

What we discovered
Talking Laughing Competing Connecting

“Before we build ideas together, we need to connect first.”
Takeaway: Connect
Day 2 18 August 2026

Discover

Know Yourself. Know Your Journey.

Day two focused on personality, purpose, and self-awareness. Through the 16 Personalities activity, we explored our strengths, preferences, and thinking styles.

Zen Pencils gave us a story that encouraged deep reflection on our individual journeys and how diverse minds build formidable startup teams.

What we explored
Strengths Preferences Thinking styles

“Different paths. Different pace. Same possibility of success.”
Takeaway: Understand Yourself
Day 3 19 August 2026

Collaborate

Who’s the Imposter?

Day three transformed interactive games into high-stakes lessons in trust, communication, and teamwork.

In a real-life version of Among Us, every team had a specific mission—except one hidden imposter. To find the imposter, we had to observe micro-behaviors, communicate clearly, build mutual trust, and decide together under time pressure. Later, the Marshmallow Challenge pushed us to iterate rapidly, listen actively, and build resilient structures collaboratively.

What teamwork required
Observe Communicate Trust Decide Listen Build Experiment Adapt
Marshmallow Challenge: Rapid team prototyping.
Innovation Lab: Collaborative problem solving.
“Great teams don’t just talk. They listen, connect and build together.”
Takeaway: Trust + Teamwork
Day 4 20 August 2026

Own

From Participants to Pioneers.

Day four introduced us to the rigorous mindset and accountability behind the programme. Mrs. Hema’s orientation introduced the seven FORGE principles—centred on mindset, discipline, and growth, along with the scoreboard to track our total ownership of results.

The inauguration of the first PRICE batch featured Rai Kumar, CEO of RAI. He showed us retail through the customer’s eyes, encouraging us to look beyond surface-level answers and step into real market problem-solving.

What we learned
Ask better questions See new perspectives Look for solutions Take ownership

Takeaway: Observe • Question • Own
Day 5 21 August 2026

Create

Entrepreneurship, Beyond Theory.

The final day marked the YEP Kick-Off on World Entrepreneurs’ Day at KCT. Ramkrishna Muthu, Managing Director of Thulasi Pharmacies, shared real-life entrepreneurial stories that revealed how authentic growth unfolds sustainably over time through grit and daily decisions.

The entrepreneurial journey
Challenge → Choice → Courage → Consistency → Growth

The biggest realisation from the session was that growth is a daily choice, not a single moment.

“Entrepreneurs aren’t built overnight. They are built through the choices they make every day.”
Takeaway: Experience • Persist • Create

Our Week in One Frame

Five days. Five lessons. One mindset.

  • 1
    Connect — Build relationships before building ideas.
  • 2
    Discover — Understand your strengths and your journey.
  • 3
    Collaborate — Trust, listen, and build with others.
  • 4
    Own — Observe, question, and take responsibility.
  • 5
    Create — Make courageous choices and create impact.

What we take forward

Question more. Listen better. Think differently. Work together. Create impact.

“We came as participants. We leave as the first batch—ready to build, experiment and make an impact.”

And this is just the beginning.
